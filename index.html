AROUND KANNUR

Places to Explore

From Asia's longest drive-in beach to ancient temples, misty hills and colourful Theyyam — discover the very best of North Kerala from Athithi.

Muzhappilangad Drive-in Beach

Asia's longest drive-in beach — drive on the sand, catch sunrises and go fishing along the serene shoreline.

St. Angelo Fort (Kannur Fort)

A historic triangular Portuguese-built seaside fort offering panoramic views of the Arabian Sea and the town.

Paithal Mala

A scenic trekking hotspot with dense forests, rich wildlife and stunning hill views on the Kerala–Karnataka border.

Palakkayam Thattu

A misty hill station offering trekking, camping and sweeping panoramas of the Western Ghats.

Payyambalam Beach

A popular Kannur beach with golden sands, coconut groves and gorgeous sunset views over the Arabian Sea.

Kavvayi Backwaters & Kayaking

Kerala's third-largest backwater — glide through serene waters with kayaking and boat savari for nature lovers.

Ezhimala Beach & Hills

The sea curves around three sides of the seven-peaked Ezhimala hills — a striking coastal landscape rich in legend.

Parassinikadavu Muthappan Temple

A famous temple dedicated to Muthappan, renowned for its daily Theyyam performances and calm setting.

Arakkal Museum

The heritage home of Kerala's only Muslim royal family, showcasing artefacts of the Arakkal dynasty.

Thalassery Fort

One of Kerala's most important former European trading centres — an atmospheric laterite fort with sea views.

Bekal Fort

Kerala's largest and best-kept fort, set dramatically on a headland with sweeping Arabian Sea views.

Ananthapura Lake Temple

The only lake temple in Kerala — a picturesque shrine set in the middle of a serene lake.

LIVING TRADITIONS

Experience Theyyam

Kannur is the living heart of Theyyam — a vibrant ritual art form where performers become divine figures through elaborate costumes, towering headdresses, fiery face paint and hypnotic drumming and dance.

Staying at Athithi places you close to celebrated Theyyam venues such as the Parassinikadavu Muthappan Temple, where you can witness this centuries-old tradition up close. We're happy to help you plan visits around the local Theyyam season and other cultural programmes and festivals.
