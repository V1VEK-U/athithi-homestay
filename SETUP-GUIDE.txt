========================================================================
 ATHITHI HOMESTAY — PUTTING THE SITE ONLINE WITH A LIVE EDITOR (FREE)
========================================================================

This site now has a built-in Content Manager at the address  /admin
Once set up, you (the owner) log in there, edit text and photos, click
Publish, and the LIVE website updates automatically for everyone —
no downloading or replacing files.

It uses: GitHub (stores the site) + Netlify (hosts it free + login).
Both are free. Total setup time: about 15 minutes, one time only.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
WHAT YOU NEED
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- An email address
- A web browser
(That's it. No coding, no payment.)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1 — Create a free GitHub account & upload the site
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to  https://github.com/join  and make a free account.
2. Click the "+" (top-right) > "New repository".
3. Name it, e.g.  athithi-homestay . Keep it Public. Click
   "Create repository".
4. On the new repo page click "uploading an existing file".
5. Open the "athithi-homestay-real" folder on your computer, select ALL
   files and folders inside it (index.html, style.css, script.js,
   images/, admin/, content/, netlify.toml, etc.) and drag them into
   the GitHub upload box.
6. Wait for them to finish, then click "Commit changes".

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2 — Host it on Netlify (free)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Go to  https://app.netlify.com/signup  and sign up with your GitHub
   account (easiest — click "GitHub").
2. Click "Add new site" > "Import an existing project" > "GitHub".
3. Authorise Netlify, then pick your  athithi-homestay  repository.
4. Leave settings as they are (no build command needed) and click
   "Deploy site".
5. In ~30 seconds your site is live at a link like
   https://athithi-homestay.netlify.app
   (You can rename it in Site settings > Change site name, or add your
   own domain like athithihomestay.com later.)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 3 — Turn on the login for the Content Manager
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The editor at /admin needs Netlify's free "Identity" + "Git Gateway":

1. In your site's Netlify dashboard, go to it and open the tab called
   Identity (newer accounts: Site configuration > Identity) and click
   "Enable Identity".
2. Under Identity > Registration preferences, set it to "Invite only"
   (so only you can log in).
3. Scroll to Services > Git Gateway and click "Enable Git Gateway".
4. Go to Identity > "Invite users" and invite YOUR email address.
5. Check your email, click the invite link, and set your password.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 4 — Edit your website anytime
------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. Visit  https://YOUR-SITE.netlify.app/admin
2. Log in with the email + password you just created.
3. You'll see friendly forms:
     - "Website Content" — edit hero text, About, Rooms, Attractions,
        Reviews, Contact details, and the Gallery photos.
     - Upload/replace any image right in the browser.
4. Click "Publish". Your live website updates in under a minute.

That's it — no files to download, no uploads, changes are live for
every visitor.
